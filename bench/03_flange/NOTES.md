# 03 flange

A revolved solid plus a polar pattern: checks cylinder booleans and circular
patterns on both kernels.

- Outer diameter Ø60, thickness 10
- Centre bore Ø20
- 6 x Ø6 holes, evenly spaced on a Ø46 bolt circle, starting at 0°
- Bounding box 60 x 60 x 10

Expected volume = π*(30² - 10² - 6*3²)*10 = 23436.2812 mm³

## 质心手算

质心：轴对称且 z 对称 → (0,0,0)。
